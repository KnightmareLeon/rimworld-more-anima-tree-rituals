# More Anima Tree Rituals

## Overview

Expand the mystical connection between your colony and the Anima Tree with three new rituals! Seek the tree's blessing, conjure a psychic soothe, or empower your psycasters through nature.

## Natural Blessing Bestowal

Request a blessing from the Anima Tree to gain the Natural meditation focus type, opening a new way for eligible colonists to connect with nature.

However, the tree's blessings are not guaranteed. The Anima Tree may grant its blessing, reject the request, or punish the requester with paralytic abasia. A successful ritual may also leave the requester psychically shocked.

Gather colonists to participate and improve the ritual's chances of success. Colonists with a stronger Plants skill and participants who share a connection with nature can help improve the outcome.

## Anima Soothe Conjuration

Call upon the Anima Tree to conjure a Psychic Soothe, significantly improving the mood of everyone in the region for the following days.

Colonists with the Natural meditation focus type can perform the ritual, while other nature-connected colonists can gather to participate and strengthen the conjuration.

## Psywielder Blessing Bestowal

Ask the Anima Tree to permanently enhance an eligible psycaster through an Anima Tree's Psywielder Blessing.

The blessing grants three lasting enhancements:

- +25% Psychic Sensitivity
- +20% Psychic Entropy Recovery Rate
- +30% Meditation Focus Gain

The requester must be an adult who is not psychically deaf and does not already possess the blessing. Participants must have the Natural meditation focus type and can contribute their psychic strength to the ritual.
